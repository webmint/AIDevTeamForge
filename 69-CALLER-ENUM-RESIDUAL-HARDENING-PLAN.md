  - *(a) Recommended:* extend the Phase 2.4c contract with a per-helper declared caller total taken from the `trace_path` result row count — via a SIBLING setter `declare-caller-total --helper-qn <qn> --total <N>`, NOT a new required arg on `record-fix-path-helper`.
