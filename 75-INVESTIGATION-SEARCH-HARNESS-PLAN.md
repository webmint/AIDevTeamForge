## Thesis (the maintainer's framing — it governs this plan)

For a senior developer, **gathering the right data and answering "why" is 75–80% of the work.** Once you know exactly what to do, coding is mechanical. So the framework's goal is to give the LLM a harness to be *the senior developer in search and analysis* — **with human verification in the loop, not designed out of it.**

Claude implements well when it knows exactly what to do. Knowing exactly what to do is the hard part, and it is what this plan is about.

## The reframe — rigor is the floor, curiosity is the leverage

Plan 73 (Phases 1–2, complete) made `/research` more **rigorous**: check 20, the `use` discriminator, the `--all` guard, the truncation test. Every one of those is quality control on the *output* of analysis — is the claim grounded, is the row present, is the field answered. That work is necessary and it is the floor this plan builds on.

It is not where the leverage is, and this plan says so without hedging: **the variant case was lost to a curiosity gap, and the class of gate-building plan 73 did would not have prevented it.** v2 cleared every gate the framework had and reached the wrong conclusion, on the one element that mattered most, four separate times — and plan 73's now-complete evidence arm inherits the single-commit window this plan targets, so running it on that run would not have changed the call (missing step 2).

### The standing hazard — name the structural reason, because it will recur

Gates are cheap to verify. They are pass/fail, they are testable, a reviewer confirms them, and a build report can state "515 passed / 2 skipped" as proof of work.

Search steps are not. You can force a call and record its output, but **you cannot test that the model noticed the right thing.**

So there is a constant gravitational pull toward building the part you can prove works and away from the part carrying most of the value. This plan is written inside that pull and will be executed inside it.

**The concrete form the drift will take here: Phase 1 will want to become "check 21."** A build session, wanting a green artifact, will convert a search step into a presence gate, declare it done, and ship the cheap half. The tripwire is stated in each build phase's Verify — *no new check number appears in the diff* — and the reason it is a Verify criterion rather than a note is that a note would not survive the pull. **That single criterion is the tripwire's ORIGINAL and now-superseded form; it is kept here because it names the drift's most likely shape. The three paragraphs below widen it twice, and the Verify blocks carry the widened three-part version — do not quote this sentence as the current criterion.**

**The numbering convention is NOT the boundary of the threat, and the predecessor proves it.** A `Check N` grep catches only `_cmds_render_verify.py`'s sequential naming. Plan 73 shipped **two hard-fail gates that carry no check number at all** — the `literal_archaeology` presence gate and the escalation-cite loop, both inside **`PlanSeeds._validate_cross_field`** in `_research/handoff_schema.py`, both raising `ValueError` out of `finalize-handoff`, both fully capable of failing a run.
